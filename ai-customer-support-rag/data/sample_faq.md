# Acme Store — Customer Support FAQ

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Returns
Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Keep the original packaging and proof of purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection.

## Order tracking
After dispatch, customers receive a tracking link by email. If no tracking email arrives within 48 hours of purchase, contact support@example.com with the order number.

## Support hours
Live customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM–6 PM Eastern Time. Email support is monitored daily.

## Warranty
Electronics include a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered.
