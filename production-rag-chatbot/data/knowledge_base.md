# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Returns
Customers can return unused products within 30 days of delivery. Original packaging and proof of purchase are required. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Order tracking
A tracking link is emailed after dispatch. If the tracking email has not arrived within 48 hours, customers should contact support@example.com with the order number.

## Warranty
Electronics include a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is excluded.

## Support
Live support is available Monday through Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Email support is monitored daily.
